MOTIVATION

Generative AI models are trained on massive datasets that include copyrighted works — without creator consent, knowledge, or compensation.

  • Creators not notified or compensated
  • Ownership of AI output legally ambiguous
  • Copyright law lags behind AI capabilities
  • Core fairness and attribution at stake

ETHICAL FRAMEWORKS

DUTY ETHICS
Using copyrighted work without consent is categorically wrong regardless of benefit produced.
VIRTUE ETHICS
A virtuous actor treats creators with fairness and respect — not as anonymous data sources.
UTILITARIANISM
AI benefits millions, but harm to individual creators must be weighed against those gains.

REAL-WORLD CASES

Getty Images v. Stability AI (2025)
Stable Diffusion trained on ~12.3M Getty images without consent. UK High Court found limited trademark infringement but dismissed primary copyright claims on territorial grounds.
OUTCOME: Copyright claims dismissed · Trademark: limited liability
Doe v. GitHub — Copilot (2022–ongoing)
Copilot trained on billions of lines of open-source code, reproducing it without license attribution. Court dismissed DMCA claims but allowed breach-of-license claims to proceed.
OUTCOME: DMCA dismissed · Breach-of-license ongoing
Thaler v. Perlmutter (2023)
Dr. Thaler sought copyright for AI-generated art, listing his "Creativity Machine" as sole author. Both district court and D.C. Circuit denied — human authorship is a legal requirement.
OUTCOME: Denied — AI cannot hold copyright

FINDINGS

DUTY
Unconsented training data use violates categorical duty to respect creator rights.
VIRTUE
Industry behavior lacks fairness and respect for human creative labor.
UTILITY
Broad benefit only ethical if harm-reduction for creators is built in.
LEGAL GAP
Copyright law cannot address AI authorship or training data consent.

FUTURE DIRECTIONS

01
Consent-based datasets
Require explicit creator opt-in before work is ingested into AI training pipelines.
02
Creator compensation
Establish royalty or licensing systems for creative works that contribute to model performance.
03
Training transparency
Mandate public disclosure of training dataset sources for accountability and auditability.
04
Copyright law reform
Update legal frameworks to define AI authorship and protect human creators in the generative AI era.
